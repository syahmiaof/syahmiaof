# Direction contract — Digital infrastructure

## Scope / mode
Experience homepage, Read case study, fast recruiter quick view. User's detailed aesthetic and autonomous code-first checkpoints override skill interview/comp approval defaults. No alternate creative direction is needed.

## Thesis
An editorial systems atlas: large human identity shares the opening frame with a cursor-aware robot companion. The journey follows that signal from a person to a real device, its data, and future work.

## First viewport
Warm-white SYAHMI / AOF at architectural scale on near-black graphite. Left-led type, a right-side metal robot with dark visor and emerald eyes, and sparse diagram labels. Narrow top navigation. Fine bottom rule, scroll affordance, location. One flat emerald project CTA. No decorative glass.

## Signature interaction
Greetly packet journey: six labelled DOM stages drive an exploded device and signal diagram by scroll or direct keyboard-accessible buttons. Static diagram remains visible in reduced motion and WebGL failure. Hero scroll gently separates the type and draws into the compute assembly; no scroll trapping.

## Cursor interaction — requested expansion, 1 October 2026

The hero compute module is replaced by an original procedural Three.js robot. Head, body and pupils follow the cursor with independent damping; hovering its region raises it slightly and opens an arm. An occasional blink wakes the demand renderer briefly; it otherwise settles and pauses outside the viewport or hidden tab. No model download or new dependency is needed. Touch, reduced motion and GPU failure use the static robot schematic.

Large surfaces (portrait, project previews, Greetly cover and passport) tilt with a cursor-positioned light reflection. Large headings shift subtly and turn emerald; primary/project/contact actions have magnetic displacement. Roadmap/lab rows draw an accent line and nudge their title; architecture/capability nodes enlarge on hover. These interactions share eased movement and reset on pointer exit. Reduced motion removes transforms, and touch retains ordinary native actions. This expansion keeps the typography, editorial layout, source content and slower intro-to-hero handoff.

## Layout / motion
Space Grotesk display and IBM Plex Mono metadata; responsive full-bleed editorial spreads. About is quiet with original photograph. Greetly dominates. Selected builds have individual image treatments. Roadmap is rows; capability topology is accessible category tabs. Motion is scrubbed data travel, short reveals, gentle hover feedback. Native scrolling; no Lenis needed.

## Entry sequence — requested refinement

On a fresh homepage load, a fullscreen topology connects edge, cloud, delivery and intelligence nodes around “Everything connects.” Animated packets traverse crisp SVG paths. Interface and font readiness are local browser states; the connection sequence is explicitly an authored introduction, never live infrastructure telemetry. After 2.7 seconds, a 1.8-second layered crossfade reveals the hero as the network recedes toward the compute core. Skip and Escape end it immediately. Native dialog focus protects the covered page; scrolling and focus are restored on exit. Reduced motion, stored motion preference, deep links and client-side return visits bypass it. SVG/CSS only, no additional renderer or dependency; packet animations are finite and the intro unmounts after completion. The surrounding design remains unchanged.

## Constraints / responsive
Desktop spatial scene, tablet lower DPR, mobile static DOM/SVG. Small-screen diagrams turn vertical. No generated face, unsupported performance claims, fake credential verification, empty links or pretend resume file. All links backed by public profile, repo, or email.

A GSAP timeline overlaps the outgoing network: camera arrives from a three-quarter angle, body rises and eyes wake. Pointer tracking resumes in the resting composition. Skip resolves directly to the final pose.

Greetly's architecture walkthrough becomes the primary scroll sequence on sufficiently large desktop viewports. A detailed original schematic separates optics, sensor, compute board and enclosure; the event path then connects to an explicitly illustrative attendance record. Six existing steps remain selectable by mouse or keyboard. The pinned stage lasts 1900 scroll pixels, while compact screens use direct stage selection with no pinning. Typography, project content and the surrounding identity remain intact.
